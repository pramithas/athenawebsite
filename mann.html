What Mann does

Everything a session needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Book and pay in minutes

Choose a counsellor or psychiatrist, pick a time that suits you, and pay with eSewa or Khalti. A slot is never sold twice.

Calls that hold up

Audio is tuned to survive packet loss on 3G and patchy 4G. We tested it over 300 calls on simulated bad networks before the pilot.

Private by default

Sessions are never recorded. In-call chat disappears when the call ends. Your phone number is encrypted, and providers see only your chosen name.

A note before you start

Tell your counsellor what's on your mind before the session. The note is encrypted and only your provider can read it.

Reminders that stay discreet

Mann reminds you an hour before each session. The lock screen never says what the reminder is for, which matters on a shared phone.

Check-ins and prescriptions

Track how you're doing with standard PHQ-9 and GAD-7 check-ins. Licensed psychiatrists can issue digitally signed prescriptions that any pharmacy can verify.
