Mann, our tele-counselling app

Mann मन

Mann connects people across Nepal with counsellors and psychiatrists over private audio calls. It books and takes payment with eSewa or Khalti, keeps calls clear on poor connections, and never records a session.

How we work

Small team, short loops, real devices.

  1. Understand

    We meet the people who will use it and agree what success looks like before we write code.

  2. Build in slices

    You get something working every week or two, on your own phone, not a slide deck.

  3. Test on real networks

    We try every release on slow and lossy connections and on low-end Android phones.

  4. Launch and support

    We handle the store listings, privacy policy and monitoring, then keep improving it with you.
