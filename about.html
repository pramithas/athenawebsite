About us

We build for Nepal first.

Most software is designed for fast networks and new phones. Many people in Nepal have neither. Athena Solutions builds software that works for them, then scales to everyone else.

We're named after Athena, the Greek goddess of wisdom and craft. Her olive branch is in our logo.

What we believe

Four rules we work by

Design for the real network

We test on slow, lossy connections and low-end phones before anything ships. If it only works on office Wi-Fi, it isn't finished.

Privacy is the default

We collect the least data we can, encrypt what's sensitive, and write privacy policies people can actually read.

Measure, don't guess

Decisions about quality come from tests and numbers. When the data says we were wrong, we change the product.

Talk to the people who build it

We're a small team. You work directly with the engineers writing your code, not through layers of account managers.
